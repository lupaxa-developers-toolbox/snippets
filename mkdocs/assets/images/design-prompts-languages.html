Language mark prompts

Square 1:1 Lupaxa-themed language badges for snippet cards. Regenerated via the image-generation subagent. Keep the 1024px master as <language>-raw.png and serve a 128px <language>.png.

Shared brief

Square 1:1 icon, not landscape, not portrait. Isolated circular badge only. Fill the entire canvas with flat hot-magenta #FF00FF so the magenta can be keyed to true transparency. No checkerboard, no grey, no white, no black fill outside the disc. Palette: navy #111A32, charcoal, silver, and one electric blue only: exactly #6D95D3. Not cyan, not teal, not navy-blue, not purple-blue, not ice-blue. Match the hardware badge of shell.png: circular three-dimensional metallic disc, thick polished silver beveled ring, recessed sandblasted charcoal navy face, thin #6D95D3 glow ring just inside the silver rim. Change only the center glyph. One glowing #6D95D3 neon outline of the well-known official language logo, thick readable lines at 64px, single colour, no multi-colour brand fills. Soft metal rim highlight. No drop shadow on a filled ground. No suitcase.
